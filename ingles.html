Complete the Sentences with Words from the Box

Este ejercicio ayuda a mejorar tu vocabulario y comprensión del contexto. Debes seleccionar la palabra correcta del recuadro para completar cada oración de manera coherente.

Example:
Box: happy, run, school, cat, beautiful

Sentences:
1. The ____ is sleeping on the sofa. → The cat is sleeping on the sofa.
2. She goes to ____ every day. → She goes to school every day.
3. The flowers are very ____. → The flowers are very beautiful.

Tips para resolver estos ejercicios:

  • Lee toda la oración para entender el contexto
  • Identifica si necesitas un sustantivo, verbo, adjetivo o adverbio
  • Considera el tiempo verbal y la concordancia
  • Elimina las palabras ya usadas del recuadro

Match the Tips (a-d) with the Examples

Los ejercicios de emparejamiento ayudan a conectar conceptos con sus aplicaciones prácticas.

Example:

Tips:
a) Use capital letters at the beginning of sentences
b) Add -ed to regular verbs in past tense
c) Use articles (a, an, the) before nouns
d) Subject and verb must agree in number

Examples:
1. I walked to school yesterday. → b
2. The cat is sleeping. → c
3. She loves ice cream. → d
4. Hello, my name is John. → a

True or False

Estos ejercicios evalúan tu comprensión lectora y conocimiento gramatical.

Estrategias:
• Lee el texto cuidadosamente
• Busca palabras clave en las afirmaciones
• Compara con la información del texto
• Presta atención a detalles como números y fechas

Example:
Text: "Mary goes to school every day. She likes math and science."

1. Mary is a student. → True
2. Mary loves history. → False
3. Mary attends school regularly. → True

Present Simple

El presente simple se usa para expresar hábitos, rutinas, hechos generales y verdades universales.

Estructura:

Tipo Estructura Ejemplo
Afirmativa Subject + verb (+ s/es) She works every day.
Negativa Subject + don't/doesn't + verb They don't like coffee.
Interrogativa Do/Does + subject + verb? Does he speak English?
Rules:
• Con he/she/it, añade -s o -es al verbo
• Verbos terminados en -ch, -sh, -ss, -x, -o: añade -es (watches, goes)
• Verbos terminados en consonante + y: cambia y por -ies (study → studies)
• Usa do/don't con I, you, we, they
• Usa does/doesn't con he, she, it

Present Simple vs Past Simple

Present Simple Past Simple
I work at a hospital. I worked at a hospital.
She goes to school. She went to school.
They don't eat meat. They didn't eat meat.
Do you like pizza? Did you like the pizza?

Diferencias clave:

  • Present Simple: Acciones habituales, rutinas, hechos generales
  • Past Simple: Acciones completadas en el pasado

Irregular Verbs

Los verbos irregulares no siguen la regla de añadir -ed en pasado. Deben memorizarse.

Verbos irregulares más comunes:

Infinitive Past Simple Past Participle Significado
be was/were been ser/estar
go went gone ir
have had had tener
do did done hacer
make made made hacer/crear
see saw seen ver
take took taken tomar
come came come venir
know knew known saber/conocer
get got gotten/got obtener

Past Simple

El pasado simple se usa para expresar acciones completadas en un tiempo específico del pasado.

Estructura:

Tipo Regular Verbs Irregular Verbs
Afirmativa I worked yesterday. I went to school.
Negativa I didn't work yesterday. I didn't go to school.
Interrogativa Did you work yesterday? Did you go to school?

Time expressions:

  • Yesterday (ayer)
  • Last week/month/year (la semana/mes/año pasado)
  • Two days/weeks/months ago (hace dos días/semanas/meses)
  • In 2020 (en 2020)
  • When I was young (cuando era joven)

Past Continuous

El pasado continuo describe acciones que estaban en progreso en un momento específico del pasado.

Estructura: was/were + verb-ing

Sujeto Afirmativa Negativa Interrogativa
I, He, She, It was working wasn't working Was I working?
You, We, They were working weren't working Were you working?
Usos:
1. Acción en progreso en el pasado:
"I was studying at 8 pm yesterday."

2. Dos acciones simultáneas:
"While I was cooking, she was reading."

3. Acción interrumpida:
"I was sleeping when the phone rang." (Past Continuous + Past Simple)

Comparatives & Superlatives

Los comparativos se usan para comparar dos cosas, mientras que los superlativos indican el grado máximo.

Reglas de formación:

Tipo Comparativo Superlativo Ejemplo
1 sílaba + er + est tall → taller → tallest
2 sílabas (-y) y → ier y → iest happy → happier → happiest
2+ sílabas more + adj most + adj beautiful → more beautiful → most beautiful
Irregulares - - good → better → best
bad → worse → worst
Ejemplos:
Comparativos:
• This book is more interesting than that one.
• She is taller than her sister.
• Cars are faster than bikes.

Superlativos:
• This is the most expensive car in the shop.
• He is the tallest student in the class.
• This is the best pizza I've ever eaten!

Flyer (Folleto Publicitario)

Un flyer es un documento publicitario que presenta información de manera atractiva y concisa.

Elementos de un Flyer en inglés:

  • Headline: Título llamativo y claro
  • Body text: Información principal breve
  • Call to action: Invitación a realizar una acción (Visit us!, Call now!, Register today!)
  • Contact information: Teléfono, email, dirección, redes sociales
  • Images: Fotografías o ilustraciones relevantes
  • Logo: Identidad de la marca
Frases útiles para Flyers:
• Special Offer! - ¡Oferta Especial!
• Limited Time Only - Por tiempo limitado
• Don't Miss Out! - ¡No te lo pierdas!
• Join Us Today! - ¡Únete hoy!
• Free Consultation - Consulta gratis
• Book Now! - ¡Reserva ahora!
• Visit our website - Visita nuestro sitio web
